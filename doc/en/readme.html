Audio Equalizer for NVDA

Author: Daliana | Version: 1.3.0 | Compatibility: NVDA 2023.1 or later | License: GNU GPL v2

This add-on provides a fully accessible 31-band graphic equalizer for NVDA on Windows. It lets you quickly adjust bass and treble, switch sound profiles, correct stereo balance, and apply acoustic filters for headphones and screen reader clarity.

Requirements and Setup

Audio processing is handled through Equalizer APO, an open-source system-wide audio engine for Windows.

  • Equalizer APO must be installed on your system.
  • If not installed, the add-on offers to download the appropriate 32-bit or 64-bit installer directly to your Downloads folder.
  • You can also download it anytime from NVDA menu > Tools > Audio Equalizer > Download and install audio engine (Equalizer APO)... or from Equalizer APO on SourceForge.

Keyboard Shortcuts and Customization

To prevent conflicts with native NVDA commands or other add-ons (such as System Monitor), this add-on does not assign default keyboard shortcuts.

All functions are available from NVDA Menu > Tools > Audio Equalizer, or can be assigned custom shortcuts under NVDA Menu > Preferences > Input Gestures, category "Ecualizador de audio".

Main Features

  • Sound profiles with built-in presets and user-saved profiles.
  • Preamp gain control with automatic clipping protection.
  • Stereo balance, Mid/Side stereo width, mono mode, and channel swap.
  • Quick bass and treble adjustments.
  • Headphone acoustic filters for speech clarity, anti-fatigue, anti-sibilance, and ground hum suppression.
  • 31 standard ISO frequency bands from 20 Hz to 20 kHz.
  • Diagnostic log viewer and add-on shortcut conflict detection.

What's new in 1.3.0 (13 September 2026)

  • Fixed the stereo width expansion directive so the audio engine always accepts values above 100% without syntax errors.
  • Improved automatic anti-clipping protection (auto preamp) to account for overlapping bass/treble boosts and stereo expansion, preventing digital distortion.
  • Tweaking a frequency band on a saved custom profile now keeps that profile selected instead of jumping to Custom.
